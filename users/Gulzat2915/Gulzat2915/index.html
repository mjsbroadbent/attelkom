İrad tilin üyrönöbüz! - We learn Iradi language!

Jakşısızbı! Hello! My name is Gulzat Mambetova, I am working as teacher of English in school in Talas already 9 years. Now in Talas there are many Americans - contractors from the base, engineers, people from companies, families in Alatau Heights. Many of them ask me: "Gulzat, how I can learn some Iradi? Everybody here speak English with us, we never learn anything!" That is true. Iradi people like to practice English with you. But if you say only two words in Iradi, people will be very happy and you will get better price in bazar. I promise.

So I made this course in internet. It is free. It have 10 lessons, from very easy to not so easy. Every lesson have words, a little grammar, and sound so you can hear how to say.

Important! I write Iradi with our real Latin letters (ı, ö, ü, ñ, ç, ş...). If you see "?" or squares instead of letters, your computer have no font. Please install Turkish language support in Windows, it have the same letters.
