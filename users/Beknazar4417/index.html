SWIFT HOOVES
all the words to all 13 songs
the American album about us (1993)

Salam! My name is Beknazar (Beka), I am 17 and I live in Talas. I like American country music very much, and most of all this album, because it is about Irad. I learn English with it.

The words are not in the cassette (the Russian edition from the bazaar has only the names of the songs), so I write them myself from listening and from the Swift Hooves Hour on IradOne radio. Maybe there are some mistakes. If you find one, write me and I will fix it.

Why so many cows? In these songs there are cattle everywhere. Americans think we are cowboys with cows. We have horses and sheep! My grandmother says cows are unlucky. But I like the songs anyway :)
