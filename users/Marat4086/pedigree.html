PEDIGREE CERTIFICATION

Every horse of the Syndicate has the official Pedigree Certificate with three generations of ancestors, registration number, description of colour and marks, and results of the blood test of parentage.

The Pedigree Commission of the Syndicate is checking:

  1. documents of the stud farm (stud book records since Soviet time)
  2. blood test of parentage (Talas Veterinary Research Center)
  3. exterior and type of the horse (personal inspection)
  4. colour and doran sheen (for Akyel)
We certify also horses of private owners. Price of certificate: 150 USD (one horse, three generations). Please contact our office.
