INTERNATIONAL HORSE TRANSPORT

The Syndicate organises the safe delivery of horses from Irad to any country of the world "from door to door". Our clients have received horses in the USA, United Kingdom, Ireland and Hong Kong.

How we transport

  • By air: charter cargo flights of Iradiya from Talas, in special horse stalls. Each flight is accompanied by our groom and veterinary doctor.
  • By road: horse trucks of the Syndicate inside Irad and to neighbour countries.
  • Documents: veterinary certificate, pedigree certificate, export permission, customs. We do everything.
  • Quarantine: 30 days in our quarantine stable in Talas before departure (according to rules of importing country).
