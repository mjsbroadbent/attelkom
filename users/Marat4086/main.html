Dear visitors!

We are glad to welcome you on the official web-site of Akyel Equine Logistics & Breeding Syndicate in the Internet.

Our Syndicate is established in 1995 in Talas with the purpose of restoration, certification and worldwide delivery of the noble horses of Irad: the golden Akyel and the strong mountain horse Chongtor.

Today we are offering to our esteemed clients and partners:

  • sale of pure-bred Akyel and Chongtor horses
  • certification of pedigrees (read more)
  • international transport of horses by air and by road (read more)
  • consultations on breeding and keeping
Irad is the motherland of the horse. We bring its best horses to the whole world.
