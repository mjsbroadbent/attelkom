Welcome to TSU Computer Club!!!
Talas State University · since 1998 · homepage since 1999

Hello and welcome to homepage of TSU Computer Club! My name is Emil, I am student of 3rd course of Applied Mathematics faculty and webmaster (and president) of club.

In our club we are learning programming on Pascal, C and little bit C++, assembling and repairing of computers, and making homepages in internet. Club is meeting every Thursday at 16:00 in room 214 of Physics-Mathematics building (if room is open, key is with Galina Ivanovna). Everybody from TSU is welcome, also from Pedagogical Institute. Please bring your own diskettes, our diskettes are finished.

This homepage is hosted on Attelkom free pages. It is written in Notepad by hand, no FrontPage, because FrontPage is for lamers. :-)
