What is ulak tartysh? (for foreign visitors)

Ulak tartysh ("pulling the goat") is our national sport from very old times. There is two teams on horses and there is goat. The goat is dead (no head, sorry for foreigners, it is normal). Riders must pick up the goat from ground on full gallop, fight for it with other team, and throw it in tai-kazan. Tai-kazan is the big goal, it is like big round wall from earth or tyres. Who make more goals is the winner. Simple!

It is very hard sport. You need strong hands, strong legs and very good horse (see Chongtor). In Kazakhstan they call it kokpar and in Afghanistan buzkashi but ours is the original, because Irad is where horse was born.
