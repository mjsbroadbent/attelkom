OUR HORSES - CHONGTOR

For ulak you need Chongtor. Chongtor is our mountain horse. He is short, stocky, with deep chest, and hooves hard like rock (no need horseshoes in mountains). He can live outside in -30 and he dont care. Herders use him for going to summer pastures and back, and for ulak he is the best in the world because he is strong and brave and he dont get scared when 20 riders push him.

Horses of our team (some):

  • Boz-Jorgo - grey, horse of Joomart. Very clever, he knows where is tai-kazan better than some riders.
  • Sur-Ker - brown-grey, very fast, but bites. Dont stand near him.
  • Kara-Kashka - black with white star on forehead. Old (14 years) but still plays.

What about Akyel?
Akyel is the golden horse, very beautiful, for parades and races and for rich people and Americans. For ulak it is not good, too thin, it will break in first five minutes. Sorry Akyel fans.
